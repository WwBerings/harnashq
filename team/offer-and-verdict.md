# Offer ladder and scan verdict

Proposal, 2026-10-23. Scan inputs: task, hours a week (H), how often it goes the usual way (U, out of 10).

## 1. Verdict: words, not a number

A verdict tells the owner what to do; "43" does not. Checked top-down:

| Band | Rule | Next step for the visitor |
|---|---|---|
| Too small to build for | H under 3 | Use what current software already does |
| Tidy up first | U 2 or less | Write down the steps, scan again |
| Ready to hand over | H 10+ and U 6+ | Book the audit |
| Worth a closer look | everything else, including unreadable open answers | Book the audit to find which part |

**Keep hours a year**, as their own number reflected back: "You said 12 hours a week: about 550 hours a year." Their figure x 46 weeks, not our saving: it promises nothing and makes the size concrete. No euros.

**Instead of tips and privacy checklist:** one line on what an audit checks first for this task, plus "We work inside your own accounts. Your data stays yours."

## 2. Offer ladder

**Free scan (secondary):** band, sentence, hours a year, first audit question. Withheld: which part fits, how it is built, what it costs.

**Paid audit (primary):** one process, two weeks, about three hours of client time (45-minute intake plus 20 to 50 recent real cases, anonymised is fine, which we measure). Deliverable: a two-page map with three options in hours on their own numbers, one recommended with a fixed build price and a pass mark ("95% correct on three months of your history"). Kept even without a build: that map, the privacy check for this process, and fixes they can do themselves.

**Credit:** full fee off the build if signed within 60 days.

**Price:** range €495 to €1,500 excl. VAT. **Pick €750:** an owner of a 2-50 person firm signs it alone, it sits well below a build (competitors about €1,500), and it pays for about a day and a half of real work. **Too low** (under about €400) reads as a sales call with an invoice: clients don't share real cases, the audit becomes worthless, and it anchors the build price low.

**Sign-up:** email, plus "Which job should we look at, and roughly how many hours a week go into it?" (prefilled from the scan). No payment at sign-up; the founder replies to schedule and invoices with the intake.
